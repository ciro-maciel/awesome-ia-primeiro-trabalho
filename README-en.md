## Where to look for a job

- [Gupy: como a IA ordena candidatos](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-gupy-como-a-ia-ordena-candidatos) - Gupy's explanation of how its AI ranks applications. `official`

## Rights for those starting out

What comes on the payslip and what the law guarantees.

- [CLT](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-clt) - The Consolidação das Leis do Trabalho. `official` `free`
- [Lei do estágio](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-lei-do-estagio) - Law 11.788/2008, with the rights of interns. `official` `free`
- [Lei do vale-transporte](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-lei-do-vale-transporte) - Law 7.418/1985, which created the transport voucher. `official` `free`
- [Tabela de contribuição do INSS](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-tabela-de-contribuicao-do-inss) - How much of the salary goes to INSS, per the official table. `official` `free`
- [Tabelas do Imposto de Renda 2026](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-tabelas-do-imposto-de-renda-2026) - How much of the salary goes to Imposto de Renda, per the official table. `official` `free`

## Fake job and task scam

The most common scams against people looking for their first job.

- [CERT.br: golpes, o que fazer](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-cert-br-golpes-o-que-fazer) - What to do after falling for a scam, in the CERT.br booklet. `official` `free`
- [Febraban: golpe da tarefa](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-febraban-golpe-da-tarefa) - Febraban's alert about the scam that pays you to like and then charges you. `official` `free`
- [Febraban: vaga falsa](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-febraban-vaga-falsa) - Febraban's alert about fake job openings. `official` `free`
- [Registrato do Banco Central](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-registrato-do-banco-central) - Free check of accounts, loans and Pix keys in your name, to see whether someone used your data. `official` `free`

- [ANPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaprim-anpd) - The Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados, with LGPD guides and regulations. `official` `free`
